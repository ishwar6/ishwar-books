# Lotus Steelworks (India)

The company was founded in 2012 and exports to 32 countries. Its catalogue lists 115 kitchenware lines, mostly pans and utensils.
Price for the reference 24 cm frying pan: £7.85 per unit, delivered duty paid to the UK (₹830 in the domestic list).
Delivery: 29 days to an EU warehouse; 34 days door to door to a UK warehouse.
Minimum order quantity: 1,200 units per order.
Known risks: ISO 9001 certificate expires in March 2027; two late shipments in 2026.
Samples are sent free for orders above the minimum. Payment terms are 30% on order and 70% before shipping.
