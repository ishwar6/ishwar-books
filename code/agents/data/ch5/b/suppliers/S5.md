# Brightline Housewares (Vietnam)

The company was founded in 1987 and exports to 37 countries. Its catalogue lists 216 kitchenware lines, mostly pans and utensils.
Price for the reference 24 cm frying pan: £8.30 per unit, delivered duty paid to the UK ($10.45 FOB Hai Phong).
Delivery: 33 days to an EU warehouse; 38 days door to door to a UK warehouse.
Minimum order quantity: 1,000 units per order.
Known risks: factory sits in a typhoon flood zone; new owner since 2026.
Samples are sent free for orders above the minimum. Payment terms are 30% on order and 70% before shipping.
