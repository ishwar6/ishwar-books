# Harbin Enamel Co. (China)

The company was founded in 1967 and exports to 35 countries. Its catalogue lists 198 kitchenware lines, mostly pans and utensils.
Price for the reference 24 cm frying pan: £6.10 per unit, delivered duty paid to the UK ($7.70 FOB Shanghai).
Delivery: 38 days to an EU warehouse; 41 days door to door to a UK warehouse.
Minimum order quantity: 2,000 units per order.
Known risks: coating failed one EU lead-migration test in 2025; prices quoted for orders placed before June 2027.
Samples are sent free for orders above the minimum. Payment terms are 30% on order and 70% before shipping.
