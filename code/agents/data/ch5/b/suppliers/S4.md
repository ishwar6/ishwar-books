# Vardar Kitchen (North Macedonia)

The company was founded in 2012 and exports to 16 countries. Its catalogue lists 173 kitchenware lines, mostly pans and utensils.
Price for the reference 24 cm frying pan: £9.95 per unit, delivered duty paid to the UK (€11.60 ex works).
Delivery: 6 days to an EU warehouse; 12 days door to door to a UK warehouse.
Minimum order quantity: 500 units per order.
Known risks: owner is in a contract dispute with a former distributor; no UKCA marking yet.
Samples are sent free for orders above the minimum. Payment terms are 30% on order and 70% before shipping.
