# Kestrel Cookware (Portugal)

The company was founded in 1981 and exports to 15 countries. Its catalogue lists 55 kitchenware lines, mostly pans and utensils.
Price for the reference 24 cm frying pan: £11.40 per unit, delivered duty paid to the UK (€13.30 in the euro price list).
Delivery: 4 days to an EU warehouse; 9 days door to door to a UK warehouse.
Minimum order quantity: 300 units per order.
Known risks: main kiln is down for repair until February 2027; family-owned, single factory.
Samples are sent free for orders above the minimum. Payment terms are 30% on order and 70% before shipping.
