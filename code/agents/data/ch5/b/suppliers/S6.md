# Oakmere Forge (United Kingdom)

The company was founded in 2004 and exports to 26 countries. Its catalogue lists 162 kitchenware lines, mostly pans and utensils.
Price for the reference 24 cm frying pan: £14.75 per unit, delivered duty paid to the UK (trade price before VAT).
Delivery: 8 days to an EU warehouse; 3 days door to door to a UK warehouse.
Minimum order quantity: 150 units per order.
Known risks: capacity limited to 4,000 units a month; steel surcharge may add up to 6%.
Samples are sent free for orders above the minimum. Payment terms are 30% on order and 70% before shipping.
