# Pearl River Housewares (S06)
Foshan, China

Founded in 1981, Pearl River Housewares is one of the established cookware makers of China. Our packaging is plastic-free and printed with vegetable inks; outer cartons use recycled board. Our induction-ready bases are tested on gas, ceramic, electric and induction hobs. We exhibit every year at Ambiente in Frankfurt and at the main Asian homeware fairs.

## Products
Our range covers frying pans, saucepans, chef's knives, mixing bowls and oven dishes. A dedicated export team answers trade enquiries in English within one working day. We train our own apprentices and many of our supervisors started on the factory floor. Handles are riveted with stainless steel rivets and tested to carry five times the weight of a full pan.
