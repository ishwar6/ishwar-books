# Sun Harbour Kitchen (S03)
Ningbo, China

The company was founded in 1971 as a family workshop in Ningbo. All steel is traceable to the mill, and every batch carries a certificate of composition. A dedicated export team answers trade enquiries in English within one working day. We train our own apprentices and many of our supervisors started on the factory floor.

## Products
Our range covers frying pans, saucepans, chef's knives, mixing bowls and oven dishes. Our packaging is plastic-free and printed with vegetable inks; outer cartons use recycled board. A dedicated export team answers trade enquiries in English within one working day. Our induction-ready bases are tested on gas, ceramic, electric and induction hobs.

## Quality and certification
Our packaging is plastic-free and printed with vegetable inks; outer cartons use recycled board. We believe good cookware should last for decades, so we design every handle, rivet and base to be repaired rather than replaced. Our engineers run accelerated wear tests on every coating, equal to about five years of daily home use. Certifications: ISO 9001, BSCI, SEDEX member.
