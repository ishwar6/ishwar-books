# Ganges Steel Craft (S09)
Moradabad, India

Ganges Steel Craft has made kitchenware in Moradabad since 1955. Our showroom welcomes trade buyers by appointment, and samples can be sent within a week of a request. We train our own apprentices and many of our supervisors started on the factory floor. Our packaging is plastic-free and printed with vegetable inks; outer cartons use recycled board.

## Products
Our range covers frying pans, saucepans, chef's knives, mixing bowls and oven dishes. A dedicated export team answers trade enquiries in English within one working day. Customers in more than twenty countries sell our products under their own brands or ours. We believe good cookware should last for decades, so we design every handle, rivet and base to be repaired rather than replaced.
