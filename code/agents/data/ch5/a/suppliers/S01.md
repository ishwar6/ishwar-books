# Duro Cookware (S01)
Porto, Portugal

The company was founded in 1991 as a family workshop in Porto. Our quality laboratory tests food-contact materials to the European framework regulation and to the relevant UK rules. Our induction-ready bases are tested on gas, ceramic, electric and induction hobs. Our design team works with professional chefs to test every product in real kitchens before it reaches our catalogue.

## Products
Our range covers frying pans, saucepans, chef's knives, mixing bowls and oven dishes. We exhibit every year at Ambiente in Frankfurt and at the main Asian homeware fairs. We offer private-label service with custom handles, colours and gift boxes for orders above our standard minimum. We believe good cookware should last for decades, so we design every handle, rivet and base to be repaired rather than replaced.
