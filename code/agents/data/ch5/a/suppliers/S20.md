# Tuscan Copper (S20)
Florence, Italy

The company was founded in 1995 as a family workshop in Florence. A dedicated export team answers trade enquiries in English within one working day. We publish an annual sustainability report and track the energy used per tonne of finished product. Our showroom welcomes trade buyers by appointment, and samples can be sent within a week of a request.

## Products
Our range covers frying pans, saucepans, chef's knives, mixing bowls and oven dishes. We train our own apprentices and many of our supervisors started on the factory floor. All steel is traceable to the mill, and every batch carries a certificate of composition. We exhibit every year at Ambiente in Frankfurt and at the main Asian homeware fairs.
