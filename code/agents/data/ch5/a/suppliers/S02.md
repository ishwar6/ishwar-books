# Kestrel Steelworks (S02)
Sheffield, United Kingdom

Kestrel Steelworks has made kitchenware in Sheffield since 1960. Our design team works with professional chefs to test every product in real kitchens before it reaches our catalogue. Customers in more than twenty countries sell our products under their own brands or ours. Our induction-ready bases are tested on gas, ceramic, electric and induction hobs.

## Products
Our range covers frying pans, saucepans, chef's knives, mixing bowls and oven dishes. Our packaging is plastic-free and printed with vegetable inks; outer cartons use recycled board. Handles are riveted with stainless steel rivets and tested to carry five times the weight of a full pan. All steel is traceable to the mill, and every batch carries a certificate of composition.
