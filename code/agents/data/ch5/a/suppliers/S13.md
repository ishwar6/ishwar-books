# Saigon Hearth (S13)
Binh Duong, Vietnam

The company was founded in 1953 as a family workshop in Binh Duong. Our quality laboratory tests food-contact materials to the European framework regulation and to the relevant UK rules. Handles are riveted with stainless steel rivets and tested to carry five times the weight of a full pan. We publish an annual sustainability report and track the energy used per tonne of finished product.

## Products
Our range covers frying pans, saucepans, chef's knives, mixing bowls and oven dishes. We believe good cookware should last for decades, so we design every handle, rivet and base to be repaired rather than replaced. Our design team works with professional chefs to test every product in real kitchens before it reaches our catalogue. Our engineers run accelerated wear tests on every coating, equal to about five years of daily home use.
