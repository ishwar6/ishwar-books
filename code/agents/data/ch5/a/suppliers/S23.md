# Andes Housewares (S23)
Monterrey, Mexico

Founded in 1985, Andes Housewares is one of the established cookware makers of Mexico. We publish an annual sustainability report and track the energy used per tonne of finished product. Our design team works with professional chefs to test every product in real kitchens before it reaches our catalogue. All steel is traceable to the mill, and every batch carries a certificate of composition.

## Products
Our range covers frying pans, saucepans, chef's knives, mixing bowls and oven dishes. We exhibit every year at Ambiente in Frankfurt and at the main Asian homeware fairs. We offer private-label service with custom handles, colours and gift boxes for orders above our standard minimum. Our showroom welcomes trade buyers by appointment, and samples can be sent within a week of a request.
