# Delta Cookware (S15)

Founded in 1970, Delta Cookware is one of the established cookware makers of Netherlands. We exhibit every year at Ambiente in Frankfurt and at the main Asian homeware fairs. ## Products
Our range covers frying pans, saucepans, chef's knives, mixing bowls and oven dishes. Our packaging is plastic-free and printed with vegetable inks; outer cartons use recycled board.
