# Moravia Ceramics (S14)
Brno, Czechia

The company was founded in 1993 as a family workshop in Brno. All steel is traceable to the mill, and every batch carries a certificate of composition. We publish an annual sustainability report and track the energy used per tonne of finished product. Our packaging is plastic-free and printed with vegetable inks; outer cartons use recycled board.

## Products
Our range covers frying pans, saucepans, chef's knives, mixing bowls and oven dishes. A dedicated export team answers trade enquiries in English within one working day.
