# Carpathia Kitchen (S22)
Krakow, Poland

The company was founded in 1955 as a family workshop in Krakow. Seasonal colours change twice a year, and our core range stays the same so that retailers can reorder with confidence. We exhibit every year at Ambiente in Frankfurt and at the main Asian homeware fairs. All steel is traceable to the mill, and every batch carries a certificate of composition.

## Products
Our range covers frying pans, saucepans, chef's knives, mixing bowls and oven dishes. We believe good cookware should last for decades, so we design every handle, rivet and base to be repaired rather than replaced. Handles are riveted with stainless steel rivets and tested to carry five times the weight of a full pan. Customers in more than twenty countries sell our products under their own brands or ours.
