# Bosphorus Housewares (S19)
Istanbul, Turkey

The company was founded in 1979 as a family workshop in Istanbul. All steel is traceable to the mill, and every batch carries a certificate of composition. Seasonal colours change twice a year, and our core range stays the same so that retailers can reorder with confidence. We train our own apprentices and many of our supervisors started on the factory floor.

## Products
Our range covers frying pans, saucepans, chef's knives, mixing bowls and oven dishes. We exhibit every year at Ambiente in Frankfurt and at the main Asian homeware fairs. Handles are riveted with stainless steel rivets and tested to carry five times the weight of a full pan. Our showroom welcomes trade buyers by appointment, and samples can be sent within a week of a request.
