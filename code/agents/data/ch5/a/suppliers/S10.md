# Bremer Kuche (S10)
Bremen, Germany

The company was founded in 1958 as a family workshop in Bremen. Handles are riveted with stainless steel rivets and tested to carry five times the weight of a full pan. Our induction-ready bases are tested on gas, ceramic, electric and induction hobs. We train our own apprentices and many of our supervisors started on the factory floor.

## Products
Our range covers frying pans, saucepans, chef's knives, mixing bowls and oven dishes. All steel is traceable to the mill, and every batch carries a certificate of composition. Our quality laboratory tests food-contact materials to the European framework regulation and to the relevant UK rules. We publish an annual sustainability report and track the energy used per tonne of finished product.
