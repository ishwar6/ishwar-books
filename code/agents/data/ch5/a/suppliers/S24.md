# Pacific Rim Kitchen (S24)
Kaohsiung, Taiwan

Pacific Rim Kitchen has made kitchenware in Kaohsiung since 1997. All steel is traceable to the mill, and every batch carries a certificate of composition. Our quality laboratory tests food-contact materials to the European framework regulation and to the relevant UK rules. Our showroom welcomes trade buyers by appointment, and samples can be sent within a week of a request.

## Products
Our range covers frying pans, saucepans, chef's knives, mixing bowls and oven dishes. Every pan is checked by hand for flatness and coating thickness before it is packed. Seasonal colours change twice a year, and our core range stays the same so that retailers can reorder with confidence. We train our own apprentices and many of our supervisors started on the factory floor.
