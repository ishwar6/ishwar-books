== A. Facts that survive n hand-offs, if each hop keeps a fact with probability q ==
   assumptions: hops lose facts independently, a lost fact is never recovered later, every fact is equally likely to be lost
      q     n=1     n=2     n=3     n=5     n=8    n=10
  0.990     99%     98%     97%     95%     92%     90%
  0.950     95%     90%     86%     77%     66%     60%
  0.900     90%     81%     73%     59%     43%     35%
  0.867     87%     75%     65%     49%     32%     24%
   q = 0.867 is the per-hop retention implied by our free-text chain (14.7 of 30 facts after 5 hops: (14.7/30)**(1/5))
   of 30 facts after 5 hops: q=0.95 keeps 23.2, q=0.90 keeps 17.7, q=0.867 keeps 14.7

== B. A verifier after every hop that detects a share d of the losses and gets them restored ==
   q' = q + (1 - q) * d    (assumes a detected loss is always repaired, and the verifier never removes a correct fact)
   q=0.900, d=0.50: q'=0.950; after 5 hops 59% -> 77%
   q=0.900, d=0.80: q'=0.980; after 5 hops 59% -> 90%
   q=0.900, d=0.95: q'=0.995; after 5 hops 59% -> 98%
   q=0.867, d=0.50: q'=0.933; after 5 hops 49% -> 71%
   q=0.867, d=0.80: q'=0.973; after 5 hops 49% -> 87%
   q=0.867, d=0.95: q'=0.993; after 5 hops 49% -> 97%
