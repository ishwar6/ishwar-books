# Travel and Expense Policy

*Effective 1 March 2026. Owner: Finance.*

## Booking travel

All business travel is booked through **Voyage**, our travel tool, which enforces the
limits below automatically. Trips with a total estimated cost above **₹1,00,000
(about $1,200)** need written pre-approval from the department head before booking.

## Flights

- Flights **under 6 hours**: economy.
- Flights **6 hours or longer**: premium economy or business, at the traveller's choice.
- Book at least **14 days** in advance where possible. Exceptions for customer incidents
  do not need approval but must be noted on the expense.

## Hotels

Nightly caps (room rate before taxes):

| Region | Cap |
|---|---|
| India | **₹8,000** |
| Europe | **€180** |
| United States | **$220** |
| Rest of world | $180 |

If a customer site is in a city where the cap cannot be met, book the cheapest reasonable
option and add a note in Ledger.

## Meals and per diem

Travellers receive a per diem instead of itemising meals:

- Travel within India: **₹2,000 per day**.
- International travel: **$75 per day**.

Per diem is halved on travel days shorter than 8 hours. Alcohol is never reimbursed.

## Ground transport

- Use ride-hailing or taxis for airport transfers; rental cars only with manager approval.
- Personal car mileage is reimbursed at **₹12 per km** in India and at the local
  government rate elsewhere.

## Submitting expenses

- Expenses are submitted in **Ledger** (our expense tool) within **30 days** of the spend.
  Claims older than 30 days are rejected unless Finance grants an exception.
- Receipts are required for any single item above **₹500 / $10**.
- Reimbursement is paid with the next payroll after approval, typically within 10 days.

## Corporate cards

Employees who travel more than 4 times a year may request a corporate card. Card spend
still has to be reconciled in Ledger within 30 days.

## Customer entertainment

Meals with customers are reimbursed up to **$120 per person**, with the attendee list
recorded in Ledger. Gifts to customers above **$100** need Legal approval.
