# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

*Effective 1 February 2026. Owner: People & Culture.*

## Hybrid by default

Lumora is a **hybrid** company. Employees attached to an office work from the office
**3 days per week**. The default office days are **Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday**; teams
may agree on a different pattern with their department head.

Roles that require physical presence (test hall, field service, hardware labs) are on-site
as scheduled by their manager.

## Core hours

For India-based teams, **core hours are 11:00 to 16:00 IST**; meetings that need the whole
team are scheduled inside this window. European and US offices set core hours locally and
publish them in the team's Slack channel.

## Fully remote roles

Fully remote employment (no office attachment) requires **VP approval** and is generally
limited to roles where the team is already distributed.

## Work from anywhere

Employees may work from a location other than their home country for up to **30 days per
calendar year**, with manager approval and a check with People & Culture on tax and
security constraints. Confidential data must not be accessed from public networks without
the VPN.

## Home office support

- A one-time **home office stipend of ₹15,000** (or local equivalent, about $180) for a
  desk, chair or monitor, claimed through Ledger within the first 3 months.
- A monthly **internet allowance of ₹1,500** (or local equivalent), paid with salary.
- IT provides a laptop, and on request a second monitor, keyboard and headset.

## Expectations

- Keep your Slack status and calendar accurate on remote days.
- Cameras on by default in meetings with external participants.
- Respond to direct messages within core hours on the same day.
