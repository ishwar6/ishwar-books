# Lumora Robotics - Company Overview

*Last updated: January 2026. Owner: People & Culture. Classification: Internal.*

## Who we are

Lumora Robotics builds autonomous mobile robots (AMRs) and the software that runs them
inside warehouses and fulfilment centres. The company was founded in **2017 in Pune, India**
by **Priya Raghavan (CEO)** and **Daniel Okafor (CTO)**, who met while working on a
university project on indoor navigation.

As of January 2026 we have **340 employees** across four offices:

| Office | Function | Opened |
|---|---|---|
| Pune (HQ) | Engineering, Product, Finance, People | 2017 |
| Bengaluru | Engineering (Fleet Platform), Support | 2019 |
| Rotterdam | European sales, field service, EU support | 2022 |
| Austin | North American sales, field service | 2024 |

## Mission

"Move every box without moving a person." We believe warehouse work should be planned by
software and carried by robots, so that people supervise, not lift.

## Products

Lumora sells three products that work together:

1. **Atlas** - the robot. The current model is the **Atlas A2**, an autonomous mobile
   robot that carries shelves, totes and pallets up to 250 kg. A smaller **Atlas A2 Lite**
   carries 120 kg. See the Atlas A2 specification sheet for details.
2. **Beacon** - the fleet management software. Beacon assigns tasks to robots, plans
   paths, manages charging and exposes a REST API to warehouse management systems (WMS).
   Beacon is sold per robot per month.
3. **Compass** - an analytics add-on for Beacon that shows throughput, utilisation,
   battery health and predicted maintenance. Compass is priced per robot per month on
   top of Beacon.

## Scale

- More than **6,000 Atlas robots** deployed.
- **120+ warehouses** in **14 countries**.
- Largest single site: 410 robots at a fulfilment centre near Rotterdam.

## How we work

We are organised into product groups: **Robot Platform** (hardware, firmware, navigation),
**Fleet Platform** (Beacon, Compass, APIs), **Field & Support**, and **Go-to-Market**.
Each product group has an engineering lead and a product lead. Company-wide planning is
done quarterly; engineering ships on a two-week release cadence (see the Beacon release
process).

## Values

- **Safety first.** A robot that stops is always preferred to a robot that guesses.
- **Measure, then move.** Decisions are backed by data from Compass, not opinions.
- **Own the outcome.** Engineers carry the pager for what they ship.
