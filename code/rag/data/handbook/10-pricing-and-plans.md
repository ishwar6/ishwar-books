# Pricing and Plans (Internal Price Book)

*Effective 1 April 2026. Owner: Go-to-Market. Classification: Confidential.*

Prices are list prices in USD. Regional price lists in EUR and INR are in the sales
portal.

## Hardware

| Item | List price |
|---|---|
| **Atlas A2** robot | **$48,000** |
| **Atlas A2 Lite** robot | **$31,000** |
| Charging station (one per 8 robots recommended) | $6,500 |
| Spare battery pack | $3,900 |
| Extended warranty, 24 → **48 months** | **$4,200 per robot** |

Standard hardware warranty is **24 months** from commissioning.

## Software (per robot, per month)

| Item | Price |
|---|---|
| **Beacon** fleet management | **$180** |
| **Compass** analytics add-on | **$40** |
| On-premise Beacon deployment | +$25 |

Software is billed monthly in arrears, or annually in advance.

## Discounts

- **10%** off software for **annual prepayment**.
- **15%** off software for fleets of **more than 100 robots**.
- Discounts stack (annual prepay on a 100+ fleet = 25% off software).
- Hardware discounts above 5% need approval from the VP Sales.

## Worked example

A customer with **120 Atlas A2 robots**, Beacon and Compass, paying monthly:

- Software list: 120 × ($180 + $40) = **$26,400 per month**.
- With the >100-robot discount (15%): **$22,440 per month**.

## Implementation services

- Site survey and simulation: **$12,000** per site.
- Commissioning: $900 per robot, minimum $25,000 per site.
- Training (on-site, 2 days): $4,000.

## Support

Bronze support is included with Beacon. Silver, Gold and Platinum are priced as a
percentage of the annual software fee: **10%, 18% and 25%** respectively. See the Support
SLA document for what each tier includes.

## Contract terms

- Minimum contract term: **12 months**.
- Payment terms: net 30.
- Price increases are capped at 5% per year for multi-year contracts.
