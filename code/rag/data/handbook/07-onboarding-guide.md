# New Hire Onboarding Guide

*Owner: People & Culture. Read this before your first day.*

## Before day one

IT ships your laptop to arrive **2 business days before** your start date. You will receive
an email from **Okta** to set up your identity and MFA. Your manager assigns you an
**onboarding buddy** - a peer, not your manager - who is your first stop for questions.

## Day one

1. Okta setup, then the tools you will use every day:
   - **Slack** (chat), **Jira** (work tracking), **GitHub** (code), **Hive** (HR: leave,
     payslips, goals), **Ledger** (expenses), **Voyage** (travel).
2. Security awareness training (about 45 minutes). Mandatory before any system access
   beyond email and Slack.
3. Meet your buddy and your manager; agree on your first two weeks.

## First week: Atlas Academy

Every new hire, regardless of role, attends **Atlas Academy** - a **3-day** course covering
how the Atlas robot works, how Beacon schedules a fleet, and how a customer warehouse runs
a shift. Engineers spend half a day driving robots in the Pune test hall.

## Weeks two to four

- Engineers pair with their buddy and ship a first change to production by the end of
  **week two** (usually a small fix flagged `good-first-issue`).
- Sales and Support shadow customer calls and complete the Beacon product certification.

## Check-ins

Your manager schedules **30-, 60- and 90-day check-ins**. The 90-day check-in includes a
written summary of what you have learned and what you want to focus on next.

## Probation

Probation lasts **6 months**. At the end, your manager confirms the role in Hive. During
probation you may take at most 5 days of PTO (see the PTO policy).

## Benefits summary

- Health insurance for you and dependents from day one.
- Home office stipend and internet allowance (see the Remote Work policy).
- Learning budget: **₹60,000 (about $700) per year** for courses, books and conferences,
  claimed through Ledger.

## Who to ask

| Question about | Ask |
|---|---|
| Laptop, accounts, access | IT via #it-help in Slack |
| Leave, payroll, benefits | your People partner or people@lumora.example |
| Expenses | Finance via #finance-help |
| Anything else | your buddy |
