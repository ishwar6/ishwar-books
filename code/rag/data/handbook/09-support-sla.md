# Customer Support Tiers and SLAs

*Owner: Field & Support. Applies to all Beacon contracts signed after 1 January 2025.*

## Support tiers

| Tier | Hours | P1 response | P1 resolution target | P2 response | Extras |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Bronze** | business hours, 9×5 local | **4 hours** | next business day | 1 business day | portal + email |
| **Silver** | 12×5 | **1 hour** | 8 hours | 4 hours | phone |
| **Gold** | 24×7 | **30 minutes** | 6 hours | 2 hours | phone, quarterly review |
| **Platinum** | 24×7 | **15 minutes** | **4 hours** | 1 hour | **dedicated Technical Account Manager (TAM)**, on-site spares |

Response time is measured from ticket creation to first human response. Resolution target
is the time to restore service (a workaround counts), not to root cause.

## Priority definitions

- **P1** - a site is halted or more than 5% of its robots are unusable, or a safety issue.
- **P2** - degraded throughput, a feature is unusable, no workaround.
- **P3** - a defect with a workaround; cosmetic issues; questions.

## How to contact support

- Portal: `https://support.lumora.example` (preferred; creates the ticket with the right
  site and tier automatically).
- Email: support@lumora.example.
- Phone (Silver and above): the number is printed on the site's support card and in the
  Beacon admin console.
- P1 tickets should always be raised by phone for Gold and Platinum customers.

## Escalation

If a response target is missed, the customer can escalate to the regional support manager;
Platinum customers escalate through their TAM. Internally, any P1 open for more than
2 hours is reviewed by the Head of Support.

## Spare parts

- Gold: spares shipped next business day.
- Platinum: **on-site spares kit** (wheel modules, battery, LiDAR) maintained by Lumora.

## Service credits

If monthly uptime for cloud-hosted Beacon falls below the **99.9%** SLA, the customer
receives a credit of **10%** of that month's Beacon fee; below 99.0%, **25%**.
