# Paid Time Off (PTO) and Leave Policy

*Effective 1 January 2026. Supersedes the 2024 policy. Owner: People & Culture.*

## Annual leave (PTO)

- Every full-time employee receives **24 days of PTO per calendar year**, accrued monthly
  at 2 days per month.
- Up to **5 unused days may be carried over** into the next year. Carried-over days must be
  used by **31 March**; after that they lapse.
- PTO is requested in **Hive**, the HR portal. Managers must approve or decline within
  **2 business days**; a request with no response after 2 business days is automatically
  escalated to the manager's manager.
- Requests of **10 or more consecutive working days** need approval from both the manager
  and the department head, and should be submitted at least 4 weeks in advance.

## Probation

New employees are on probation for the first **6 months**. PTO accrues during probation,
but at most **5 days** may be taken before probation ends, except for emergencies approved
by People & Culture.

## Sick leave

- **12 days of paid sick leave per year**, separate from PTO. Sick leave does not carry over.
- A medical certificate is required for absences of **3 or more consecutive days**.
- Sick leave is recorded in Hive on the day of return, or by the manager if the employee
  cannot.

## Parental leave

| Role | Paid leave |
|---|---|
| Primary caregiver | **26 weeks** at full pay |
| Secondary caregiver | **8 weeks** at full pay |

Parental leave can be taken in up to two blocks within the first 12 months after the birth
or adoption. Notify People & Culture at least 8 weeks before the planned start.

## Public holidays

Each office follows its local public holiday calendar (published in Hive every December).
Employees may swap up to **2 public holidays** per year for other days of religious or
cultural importance with manager approval.

## Blackout periods

The **last week of each quarter** is a PTO blackout period for the **Sales and Support**
teams only. Engineering has no blackout periods.

## Unpaid leave and sabbaticals

After **4 years** of service, employees may request an unpaid sabbatical of up to
**3 months**. Requests need VP approval.
