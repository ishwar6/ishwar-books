# Employee FAQ

*Maintained by People & Culture. Some entries may lag behind the policies they summarise -
the policy document always wins.*

**Q: How many days of PTO do I get?**
A: 20 days per year. *(Updated February 2024.)*

**Q: Where do I request leave?**
A: In Hive. Your manager gets a notification and approves it there.

**Q: How do I claim expenses?**
A: Submit them in Ledger with receipts. Claims must be in within 30 days.

**Q: What is the hotel cap for travel in India?**
A: ₹8,000 per night before taxes.

**Q: How do I book a flight?**
A: Through Voyage. It applies the flight class rules automatically.

**Q: Do I have to be in the office?**
A: Yes, three days a week, unless your role is fully remote (which needs VP approval).

**Q: What is my learning budget?**
A: ₹60,000 per year, claimed through Ledger.

**Q: I think I clicked a phishing link. What do I do?**
A: Email security@lumora.example right away - within an hour - and tell your manager. You
will not get in trouble for reporting.

**Q: Who do I contact about my laptop?**
A: IT, in the #it-help Slack channel.

**Q: How fast does an Atlas A2 go?**
A: Up to 2.0 metres per second, and 0.8 m/s in zones shared with people.

**Q: How long does it take to charge an Atlas?**
A: A full charge takes 90 minutes; a 20-minute top-up gives about 2 hours of runtime.

**Q: How often does Beacon ship a release?**
A: Every two weeks, on a Tuesday.

**Q: What is the Beacon API rate limit?**
A: 600 requests per minute per API key.

**Q: Can I use ChatGPT or Claude for work?**
A: For Internal data, yes, with the approved tools in the IT catalog. Not for Confidential
or Restricted data unless the tool has passed a vendor security review and you are using a
company account.
